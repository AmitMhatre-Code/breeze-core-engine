# Breeze Modern User Guide

Breeze Modern is a trading dashboard for your **ICICI Direct** account. It runs on your own server and talks to ICICI through the Breeze API. This guide walks through every page and every control in the app, in the order you are likely to meet them.

**New here?** Start with [Before you begin](before-you-begin.md), then [Registering your account](registering.md) and [Signing in](signing-in.md). Once you are in, [Finding your way around](finding-your-way.md) explains the parts of the screen that appear on every page.

**Looking for one feature?** Use the contents below or the sidebar. Every page of the app has its own section, and the **Topics** part pulls together subjects that span several pages, such as margins, backtests and ICICI's API limits.

> [!NOTE]
> Nothing in this guide is investment advice. Breeze Modern is a tool: how and whether you use its proposals, signals and bots is your decision. Options trading carries substantial risk, including losses larger than the premium you receive when you sell options.
