# Bug audit — 2026-09-28

A code-reading audit of the backend and the key frontend helpers, done after the stale-price bot incidents. Fixed findings are marked in the table and in their entry; everything else is still open. Each entry says what happens, where, and why it looks like a bug rather than a design choice. Everything was checked against `guide/technical/design-decisions.md` so deliberate behaviour (serialized broker calls, fail-closed licensing for *new* trades, advisory shedding, and so on) is not reported.

## How this was done

- Read the money paths end to end instead of relying on the tests: WebSocket feeds and tick pipeline, the quote router and chain builder, the P&L engine and Strategy Group (SG) dispatch/lifecycle, all five bots, the ICICI transport and pacer, licensing and heartbeat, reference data, and the frontend order and portfolio helpers.
- Four findings were reproduced with throwaway pytest probes (B-52 added on 2026-09-29) run against the real modules (in-memory Redis, temp SQLite). The probes are not in the repo; their output is in [Runtime probes](#runtime-probes).
- "Confidence" means: **Confirmed** = reproduced at runtime; **High** = follows directly from the code; **Medium** = depends on broker or deployment behaviour that could not be observed locally (there is no live ICICI access off the static IP), with the assumption stated.

## Severity

- **Critical** — can open an unintended position (including a naked short) without the user acting.
- **High** — can leave a position unprotected, or drive trades and stops off wrong prices, in plausible conditions.
- **Medium** — degrades protection, sizing or data correctness in specific but realistic conditions.
- **Low** — edge cases, bookkeeping, noise, or hardening.

### B-01 · Iron Fly and CAS Bingo re-send closing orders for legs already closed or never filled

> **Status: Fixed 2026-09-29** (not yet committed). See [Fix](#b-01-fix) below. The "Where" line numbers are from the code as audited, before the fix.

**Where:** [iron_fly_bot.py:1039 `_close_live`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/iron_fly_bot.py#L1039), [iron_fly_bot.py:907 `_abort_live_entry`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/iron_fly_bot.py#L907), [cas_bingo/execution.py:462 `close_live`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/cas_bingo/execution.py#L462), [cas_bingo/execution.py:352 `_abort_live_entry`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/cas_bingo/execution.py#L352), [scalping/runtime.py:760 `_exit_only_bots`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/runtime.py#L760)

**What happens:**
- When `_close_live` cannot close every leg, it writes `detail["exit_partial"] = {closed, stuck}` and leaves the cycle open. It never removes the closed legs from `cycle.legs`.
- When an entry fails and the unwind leaves anything "stuck", `_abort_live_entry` leaves the cycle open with `legs=plan.as_legs()`: every *planned* leg at the full planned quantity, including legs that never filled.
- The bot is disarmed, but `_exit_only_bots` keeps ticking any bot with an open live cycle. The next exit verdict re-runs `_close_live` over `cycle.legs`. Hard square-off, window end, drift, stop or stale feed all produce one.
- That buys back shorts that were already bought back, which opens new longs. It sells wings that were already sold or never bought, which opens **new naked shorts**.
- CAS Bingo behaves the same way for `exit_partial` cycles, because they carry `net_entry_per_unit`, so `evaluate_exit` still produces a verdict. At expiry, `settle()` also books P&L over every leg in the cycle, including legs never held or already closed.

**Why it is a bug:** The comments say the open row is kept "so the exit loop keeps trying to manage it". But the data the loop manages is the *intended* structure, not what is actually held. Nothing reconciles `cycle.legs` with what filled or closed.

**Trigger:** Any exit or unwind where one leg is rejected, throttled past its attempts, or not filled at the ladder's price. These are plausible in fast markets and on thin wings.

<a id="b-01-fix"></a>
#### Fix (2026-09-29)

Recorded as [design-decisions #53](../guide/technical/design-decisions.md#53-a-live-multi-leg-cycles-legs-are-what-it-holds-now-and-a-close-that-sticks-is-retried-only-against-the-brokers-positions). The fix enforces one rule: **`cycle.legs` is what the bot holds right now**, rewritten every time the broker answers a close or an entry unwind. It is never the plan. Both bots share one new module, [scalping/held_legs.py](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/held_legs.py).

**How a close pass works now** ([`run_close`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/held_legs.py#L107)):
- Legs close shorts first, then longs.
- The remaining size of each leg is its quantity minus `filled_quantity`. `FillResult.ok` is true only for a complete fill, so the old code recorded a close that filled 50 of 75 as 75 still open.
- A long is held back while the short of the same right is still open. The old loops went on to sell the wing after its short stuck, which opened a naked short.
- P&L is booked per leg against that leg's entry price, taken from the leg or from `entry_fills`. When everything closes in one pass, this equals the old whole-structure formula.

**When legs are left open** ([`record_stuck`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/held_legs.py#L178)):
- The remaining legs and the cycle detail are written in one transaction ([`repo.update_cycle_holdings`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/repositories/bots.py#L893)).
- The detail records:
  - `unwinding: true`
  - the P&L already banked: `realized_gross`, `realized_charges`, `closed_legs`
  - the exit that started the close (`exit_decision`), so the cycle is still booked as that stop or target
  - `unwind_attempts` and `unwind_next_at`
- An aborted entry keeps only the legs that filled, at their filled size and price. Legs that never filled are dropped, `pending` is cleared, and the entry charges are stored so the final booking includes them.

**What the exit loop does with an unwinding cycle:**
- It is never judged again as the original structure. Iron Fly's [`inspect_position`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/iron_fly_bot.py#L575) and CAS Bingo's [exit loop](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/cas_bingo/runtime.py#L327) skip the target, stop and drift checks and return the new verdict `closing_remainder`.
- Every retry goes through [`legs_to_retry`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/held_legs.py#L324):
  1. It waits for the back-off: 30, 60, 120, then 300 seconds.
  2. It reads the broker's positions (`positions_for_underlying`) and cuts each leg to what the broker still holds on that side. A leg is never grown.
  3. If positions cannot be read, it sends nothing and asks again after 30 seconds (the B-11 lesson: unreadable is not flat).
  4. If the broker shows none of the legs, the cycle closes with the new reason `closed_outside_bot` and books only the P&L already banked ([`close_flat`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/held_legs.py#L366)).
- Retries are bounded ([`MAX_CLOSE_RETRIES`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/held_legs.py#L43), a code constant). After 4 failed retries, or at once if a cancel failed (an order that may still fill), the bot stops sending orders. It keeps reading positions every 5 minutes and closes the row once you have flattened the legs.
- CAS Bingo's settlement at expiry ([`_settle_remainder`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/cas_bingo/execution.py#L580)) values only the legs still held, plus the P&L already banked.

**Telegram** (nobody watches the bots live, so each step is reported there):

| Event | Message |
|---|---|
| A close or entry unwind gets stuck | "needs checking": the open legs, plus the retry plan (up to 4 tries over about 9 minutes, positions checked before each try) |
| A retry closes the leftover legs | "leftover legs closed", with the gross P&L |
| The legs are found closed by hand | "leftover legs closed": the bot has stopped managing them |
| Retries run out | "still could not be closed", with no retry promise |
| A cancel failed | the first alert, without the retry plan |

**Rows stuck by the old code:** at startup, [`repair_legacy_rows`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/held_legs.py#L476) rebuilds any open live Iron Fly or CAS Bingo cycle from its `exit_partial.stuck` or `stuck_legs` record and marks it `unwinding` with no wait. It runs from both bots' `reconcile_on_startup`. Its first retry goes through the broker check, which also corrects the old over-stated quantities.

**Files changed:**
- New: `scalping/held_legs.py`.
- `iron_fly_bot.py`: `_abort_live_entry`, `_unwind_legs`, `_close_live`, `inspect_position`, `_alert_stuck`.
- `cas_bingo/execution.py`: `_abort_live_entry`, `close_live`, `settle`, `alert_stuck`.
- `cas_bingo/runtime.py`: `_manage_open_cycles`, `reconcile_on_startup`.
- `scalping/runtime.py`: `reconcile_on_startup`.
- `repositories/bots.py`: `update_cycle_holdings`.
- `domain/bots.py`: two new reason codes, `CLOSING_REMAINDER` and `CLOSED_OUTSIDE_BOT`.
- No new environment variables.

**Tests:** 14 in the B-01 section of `backend/tests/test_scalping_iron_fly_live.py`, 3 in the B-01 section of `backend/tests/test_cas_bingo.py`. Between them they cover:
- the stuck pass keeping only what is held

**Still open:**

### B-02 · A crash mid-entry leaves a "pending" cycle that the exit path later trades as if it were filled

**Where:** [momentum_bot.py:741](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/momentum_bot.py#L741), [iron_fly_bot.py:846](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/iron_fly_bot.py#L846), [cas_bingo/execution.py:275](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/cas_bingo/execution.py#L275), [guards.py:194 `reconcile_pending_cycles`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/guards.py#L194), [guards.py:308 `has_unresolved_intent`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/guards.py#L308)

**What happens:**
- Live entries write the cycle first with `detail.pending=True, order_ids=[]`. Order ids are only recorded in `mark_cycle_placed` *after* `place_and_confirm` returns, which includes the fill wait and any cancel.
- If the process dies in that window, the row has no order ids. The deployment has documented OOM kills (#41) and portal recreates. A DB exception in the same window has the same effect.
- At startup `reconcile_pending_cycles` can only send an orphan alert. The row stays pending and is re-alerted on every restart. `has_unresolved_intent` then blocks entries **permanently**; there is no date filter, although the docstring says "for the session".
- Worse, a pending row is an open cycle, so the scalper snapshot reports `has_open_position=True` and `inspect_position` manages it. At hard square-off, stale feed or window end, `_close_live` trades the **planned** legs with `paper=False`.
  - The Long Scalper sells the planned quantity. If the buy never filled, that is a naked short.
  - The Iron Fly buys back two shorts and sells two wings that may never have been bought.

**Why it is a bug:** The design (#32/#43 style "row before order") exists so a crash leaves "a question startup can answer". Because the ids are written late, the question can never be answered. Also, CAS Bingo's exit loop skips pending rows ([cas_bingo/runtime.py:313](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/cas_bingo/runtime.py#L313)) but the scalpers' does not.

<a id="b-02-fix"></a>
#### Fix (2026-09-29)

> **Status: Fixed 2026-09-29** (not yet committed), together with [B-21](#b-21), [B-10](#b-10) and [B-52](#b-52), which share the fix. The "Where" line numbers above are from the code as audited.

Recorded as [design-decisions #54](../guide/technical/design-decisions.md#54-a-live-entrys-outcome-is-settled-from-the-broker-before-the-row-is-traded-and-a-lost-answer-is-never-read-as-a-refusal). The fix enforces one rule: **a live row is traded only once its entry outcome is known.** It covers the Long Scalper, Iron Fly and CAS Bingo. The shared code is the new module [scalping/order_intents.py](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/order_intents.py).

**What changed:**
- **Orders are journaled on the row.** Each order goes into `detail.intents` just before it is sent. Its id is added the moment ICICI returns it, before the fill wait. Entry-unwind orders are journaled too.
- **A lost answer is looked up, not assumed** (B-21). An SDK exception, a non-JSON body or a 200 without an order id is marked `outcome_unknown`. `place_and_confirm` then reads the order book once for an order with the same contract, side and quantity, placed from 60 s before to 180 s after the send. It acts on what the book shows, and stands down if the book cannot say.
- **A failed cancel or an unknown outcome freezes the entry.** The row stays `pending`; it is not unwound and not left open with its planned legs. This was a second route into the B-02 state, and it was not in the audit.
- **`resolve_pending` settles pending rows** at startup (every bot holding an open live row, not only armed ones) and on every pass. It reads every journaled order. A still-resting entry order is cancelled on its own exchange (B-10). Fills are netted per strike, then:

  | Outcome | Result |
  |---|---|
  | Nothing held | Abandoned as `entry_unfilled`, not a loss |
  | The whole structure, or the Long Scalper's leg at any size | Adopted with real entry prices and `entry_value` |
  | Part of a multi-leg structure | B-01's `unwinding` state holding only what filled |
  | Unknown | Stays pending, re-asked at 30/60/120/300 s, one Telegram alert. The bot is switched off only after 4 more failed checks. |
- **Pending rows are invisible to the exit path.** Both scalpers' `inspect_position` return nothing for one, and both `_close_live` refuse one.
- **`entry_value` is written with the fills** (B-52), in the same `mark_cycle_placed` write as the legs and detail, through one entry recorder per bot shared with adoption. Open rows from the old build are backfilled at startup.
- **The Long Scalper's exit now uses `held_legs`** (new finding, overlaps [B-23](#b-23)'s third bullet). A partial exit used to leave the full size on the row, so the next pass sold it again and went short. The same happened after a lost exit answer. Now the remainder stays, retries are back-off-gated and broker-checked, the alert is sent once, and an unknown exit halts orders.

**Telegram** (kind `scalping_orphan`): "needs checking" (first unsettled check), "switched off" (retry budget spent), "interrupted entry recovered", "interrupted entry is being closed", "nothing was traded" (only after an alert).

**Files changed:**
- New: `scalping/order_intents.py`.
- `scalping/live.py`: `place_and_confirm`, `cancel`, `FillResult`.
- `scalping/held_legs.py`: `ClosePass.halted`, `entry_price`, `alert_resolved`, `still_disarmed`.
- `momentum_bot.py`, `iron_fly_bot.py`, `cas_bingo/execution.py`: entry, entry recorder, `adopt_recovered`, pending guards.
- `scalping/runtime.py`, `cas_bingo/runtime.py`: the resolver, run at startup and on every pass.
- `scalping/guards.py`: `reconcile_pending_cycles` removed.
- `repositories/bots.py`: `mark_cycle_placed(entry_value, legs)`, `backfill_live_entry_values`.
- `processor.place_order`: the `outcome_unknown` tag.
- `guide/user/bots.md`: new safety-rail bullet.
- No new environment variables.

**Tests:**
- `test_scalping_live.py`: the reconciliation section rewritten, plus B-21, B-10, B-52 and Long Scalper exit-remainder tests.
- `test_scalping_iron_fly_live.py`: the cancel-failure test replaced by an unaccounted-order test covering both routes, plus a B-52 test.
- `test_cas_bingo.py`: resolver adoption, and a SENSEX cancel on BFO.

**Still open:**

### B-03 · An SG's own exit fill is treated as manual intervention while its other legs are still being placed

**Where:** [squareoff_dispatcher.py:298 `_handle_group_rule_hit`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/squareoff_dispatcher.py#L298), [strategy_group_lifecycle.py:159](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/strategy_group_lifecycle.py#L159), [strategy_group_lifecycle.py:308 `_handle_foreign_order`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/strategy_group_lifecycle.py#L308), [squareoff_rules.py:377 `mark_fired`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/repositories/squareoff_rules.py#L377)

**What happens:**
1. The dispatcher marks the rule `triggered`, then places legs one at a time. Placement is serialized and freeze-sliced, so this takes seconds.
2. The order ids are only persisted at the end, by `mark_fired` or `mark_fire_failed`.
3. Meanwhile the order feed delivers the first leg's fill. `on_order_notification` finds the `triggered` rule, whose `leg_results` is still `NULL`, so `order_ids_for_rule(rule)` is empty.
4. The fill is therefore classed as foreign and the rule is **Reset** with "you traded NIFTY 26000 CE outside this rule". That also releases the WS pin and sends a Reset Telegram alert.
5. Any later chunk that hits a throttle checks `_still_firing`, sees `reset`, and **stops retrying**. The position is left half-closed.
6. `mark_fired` and `mark_fire_failed` have no status guard, so they overwrite the Reset afterwards.

**Why it is a bug:** Order identity is the lifecycle's discriminator ("our exit vs the user's trade"), but the identity isn't known to the lifecycle until after the fills it is supposed to classify. Only the reconcile path is tested for `triggered` (`test_triggered_rule_is_not_reset`), not the notification path. Reproduced; see [probe 1](#runtime-probes).

**Note:** [B-19](#b-19) may currently *mask* this for the production user, because the lookup by `n.user_id` may miss. Fixing B-19 alone would expose B-03.

### B-04 · The chain builder serves ticks up to ~4 minutes old as fresh `websocket` quotes

**Where:** [chain_build_service.py:36 `_read_raw_tick`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/chain_build_service.py#L36), [chain_build_service.py:63 `_normalize_and_cache_cell`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/chain_build_service.py#L63), [ws_tick_normalize.py:171](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/ws_tick_normalize.py#L171), [quote_source_router.py:1325 `row_is_live`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/quote_source_router.py#L1325)

**What happens:**
- Every rebuild re-reads each contract's raw tick. The chain-builder rebuilds every ~2 s, and request paths such as `wait_for_strike_quote` rebuild on every poll.
- The raw key has a 120 s TTL (`WS_RAW_QUOTE_TTL_SECONDS`, which defaults to 120 in `core/config.py`, not the 300 the architecture doc states).
- `_read_raw_tick` throws away the stored `received_at`. `normalize_icici_tick(raw)` is called without `updated_at`, so the cell is stamped `time.time()` and re-written to `ws_quote_key` with a fresh 120 s TTL.
- So a contract that stopped ticking keeps appearing as a just-updated `websocket` cell for up to ~120 s. The cell then survives up to another ~120 s.
- `row_is_live()` and the scalpers' `Quote.live` check only `quote_source == "websocket"`, never the age.

**Consequences:**
- Bots enter, mark, trail and stop on minutes-old prices, with a feed that looks healthy.
- Order pricing is affected too: `wait_for_strike_quote` says it rebuilds "so an aggressive limit order is not priced off a two-second-old book", but the rebuild just re-stamps the same old tick.
- The Portfolio LTP override in `get_positions` prefers the websocket cell over the broker's own LTP.
- The UI's "Live · WebSocket" badge and `quote_as_of` are fooled as well.

This is the most direct remaining cause of "bots working off stale prices". Reproduced; see [probe 2](#runtime-probes).

<a id="b-04-fix"></a>
#### Fix (2026-09-30)

> **Status: Fixed 2026-09-30.** B-04 only; [B-24](#b-24), [B-23](#b-23) and [B-44](#b-44) are still open. [B-39](#b-39) and [B-05](#b-05) were fixed later the same day (see [B-05's fix](#b-05-fix)). The "Where" line numbers above are from the code as audited.

The fix enforces one rule: **a websocket cell carries the time its tick arrived, and a rebuild never makes it younger.**

**What changed:**
- `_read_raw_tick` returns the tick together with its stored `received_at`. A payload without one is a miss.
- `_normalize_and_cache_cell` passes `received_at` as the cell's `updated_at`, and writes the cell with only the life the tick has left: `WS_RAW_QUOTE_TTL_SECONDS` (120 s) minus its age. A rebuild no longer renews it.
- A tick already past that age builds no cell (`stale_raw` in the build stats).
- The router's single-contract read (`_fetch_cell_from_cache`) checks the cell's own time with `_ws_cell_expired`, not only the key's presence, and falls through to the stand-ins when it is too old. This is the check that still holds if a TTL is not honoured.

**What an old quote does now:**
- A contract is a `websocket` quote for 120 s after its last tick, not up to ~240 s, and `updated_at` and `quote_as_of` show the tick's real time throughout.
- After 120 s the quote is not dropped without a replacement. The router answers from snapshot, bhavcopy or REST, each labelled with its own `quote_source`, exactly as it already did at ~240 s.
- Bots read that as not live and pause (#52). The Portfolio LTP keeps the broker's figure on NFO and blanks on BFO.
- PB/SL rules are not affected. The P&L engine reads its own quote hash, which is stamped when the tick arrives, is kept until the next session and is judged on age (#27).

**Files changed:** `chain_build_service.py` (`ws_cell_max_age_seconds`, `_read_raw_tick`, `_normalize_and_cache_cell`), `quote_source_router.py` (`_ws_cell_expired`, `_fetch_cell_from_cache`). No new environment variables and no UI change.

**Tests:** three in the B-04 section of `backend/tests/test_chain_builder_worker.py` (probe 2 as a real test, a second rebuild not extending the cell, a tick past its life building nothing) and one in `backend/tests/test_quote_source_router.py`.

**Still open:**
- ~~`received_at` is stamped when the tick is written to Redis, not when it arrives ([B-39](#b-39)), so it can be late under backlog.~~ Fixed with [B-05](#b-05-fix).
- Bots still treat any `websocket` quote as live for the full 120 s; there is no tighter bound for entries, marks or stops.
- `cas_bingo.market.auction_probe` logs from before this fix show re-stamped ages and are not evidence for #50's closing-auction question.

### B-05 · One Redis error kills the tick-cache thread for the life of the process

**Where:** [ws_tick_pipeline.py:379 `_cache_loop`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/ws_tick_pipeline.py#L379), [ws_tick_pipeline.py:394](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/ws_tick_pipeline.py#L394)

**What happens:**
- `_cache_loop` has no `try/except` around `cache_set_json`. The Redis client uses `socket_timeout=2`, so one timeout is enough to raise; so is a connection reset while the `breeze-redis` sidecar is recreated, or a BGSAVE fork stall on a small instance.
- The exception ends the `ws-tick-cache` thread. `_started` stays `True`, so neither `start_tick_pipeline()` nor `reconnect_ws()` ever restarts it.
- From then on, raw ticks stop reaching Redis. The chain builder has nothing new; websocket cells expire; chains fall back to snapshot, bhavcopy or REST stand-ins; bots pause; and the quote snapshot stops capturing.
- The price watchdog reads the same raw keys, so it sees silence. It re-subscribes successfully (the socket is fine), so per [B-17](#b-17) it never escalates. Nothing recovers until the process restarts.
- The P&L engine is unaffected, because its flush loop has its own error handling.

Reproduced; see [probe 3](#runtime-probes).

<a id="b-05-fix"></a>
#### Fix (2026-09-30)

> **Status: Fixed 2026-09-30** (not yet committed), together with [B-39](#b-39), [B-17](#b-17) and [B-28](#b-28). The "Where" line numbers above are from the code as audited. Design record: `guide/technical/design-decisions.md` #56.

The fix enforces one rule: **a fault on our side of the socket never ends the tick pipeline, and is never read as ICICI going quiet.**

**What changed in the pipeline (B-05, B-39):**
- `_cache_loop` catches every error per batch. A failed batch is dropped, not retried, because the next tick for each contract is already on its way. Failures are counted and logged at most once every 30 s, and the thread waits 0.5 s so a refused connection does not spin.
- A batch is one Redis round trip (`_write_raw_batch`): one pipelined SET per contract and one dirty notice for the whole batch. A stalled Redis now costs one 2 s timeout per batch, not one per key. The chain builder is the only subscriber and never read the notice's content.
- `received_at` is stamped in `ingest_tick`, when the tick arrives, and travels with it through both queues. The raw key is written with only the life the tick has left, and a tick already past `WS_RAW_QUOTE_TTL_SECONDS` is not written. The quote snapshot's cell carries the same time.
- The process queue holds 8 batches, down from 256. When it is full the drain thread keeps ticks in the coalesce map, where the newest tick per contract replaces the older one. Nothing is dropped and nothing stale is queued.
- `start_tick_pipeline()` restarts a dead thread (`revive_tick_pipeline`) instead of returning on `_started`. `reconnect_ws()` reaches it, and so does every watchdog pass.
- Both threads are handed their queues and stop event as arguments, so a thread that outlives `stop_tick_pipeline()`'s join cannot pick up the next start's.
- `/metrics/runtime` shows `cache_alive`, `drain_alive`, `cache_errors`, `last_cache_error`, `last_cache_write_age_seconds`, `last_ingest_age_seconds`, `stale_ticks_skipped` and `thread_restarts`.

**What changed in the watchdog (B-05, B-17):**
- Each silence pass first repairs the pipeline and asks whether its writes are failing (`_pipeline_fault`). If they are, the pass re-subscribes nothing: the raw keys it reads are not being written, so their silence says nothing about ICICI.
- A rebuild is now also triggered by two passes in a row whose re-subscribe was **accepted** while nothing at all reached the process (`_ingest_silent`, read from the pipeline's arrival clock, not from Redis). One quiet chain cannot trigger it: every feed on the socket, index spot included, must be silent. The existing 5-minute cooldown applies.

**What changed in the other loops (B-28, and one more):**
- The reference-data scheduler's tick runs inside a `try`, as the SPAN scheduler's already did.
- The Telegram claim loop catches a failed pass and backs off. A link-token check that fails now reads as "keep claiming", not as "nothing outstanding".
- Not in the audit: the chain builder's pubsub thread ended on its first Redis error and logged it at debug level, leaving the worker poll-only until restart. It now re-subscribes with a 1–30 s back-off and logs a warning.

**What a Redis outage does now:** bots still pause and the feed alerts (#51) still fire while it lasts. When Redis answers again the next batch lands and everything resumes, with no restart.

**Files changed:** `ws_tick_pipeline.py`, `ws_price_feed_watchdog.py`, `redis_client.py` (`publish` on the in-memory pipeline), `reference_data/scheduler.py`, `telegram_link_portal.py`, `workers/chain_builder.py`. No new environment variables and no UI change.

**Tests:** `test_ws_tick_pipeline.py` (probe 3 as a real test, dead-thread restart, arrival timestamps, one round trip per batch, conflation under backlog), `test_ws_price_feed_watchdog.py` (`TestAcceptedButSilent`, `TestPipelineFault`), and one each in `test_reference_data_startup.py`, `test_telegram_link_portal.py` and `test_chain_builder_worker.py`.

**Still open:**
- A process that started while Redis was down stays on its in-memory store ([B-32](#b-32)).
- A scheduled reference-data load that raises is logged but not retried that day ([B-27](#b-27)).
- The accepted-but-silent rebuild has not been seen live. Look for `re-subscribes were accepted and no tick has arrived` in the next log bundle that covers a silent stretch.
- `test_telegram_link_portal.py::test_no_claim_when_no_token_is_outstanding` fails when that file is run alone, before and after this change, because it reads approval tokens from the local database.

### B-07 · ICICI REST calls have no timeout while the per-user broker lock is held

**Where:** breeze_connect `make_request` (installed SDK, `breeze_connect.py` ~L1139), [requests_patch.py:216](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/core/requests_patch.py#L216), [requests_patch.py:248](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/core/requests_patch.py#L248), [icici_api_pacing.py:402](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/icici_api_pacing.py#L402)

**What happens:**

### B-09 · Licence read-only mode blocks stop-loss exits and other risk-reducing actions

**Where:** [squareoff_dispatcher.py:311](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/squareoff_dispatcher.py#L311), `require_trading_not_revoked` on `/book/cancel-one`, `/book/cancel-commit`, `DELETE /squareoff-rules/{id}`, `POST /squareoff-rules/{id}/cancel-orphan-orders`, `DELETE /gtt-exit-orders/{id}`, `PATCH /bots/config` and parked-order deletes. **What happens:**
- When the licence reads as read-only, a breached SG is marked fire-failed and **no exit orders are sent**. The user also cannot cancel orders (including orphans that would *open* a contra position), disarm an SG, cancel a GTT, or switch a bot off.
- Read-only is not only a revoked licence. It is also "no verified heartbeat for 2× the interval", and "no heartbeat verified yet" at startup.
- The heartbeat loop waits a full interval after a failure. The staleness boundary is exactly 2× the interval, so a *single* failed heartbeat lands the next attempt just past the boundary. That produces a short read-only window, longer if the loop is blocked ([B-08](#b-08)). Two failures give up to a full interval (5–60 min).

**Why it is a bug:** The code states the opposite principle elsewhere. `scalping/decide.py`, rule 1: "a gate that blocks *entering* must never block *leaving*… would strand a live position with no stop running the moment a licence lapsed." #16's fail-closed rationale is about not *enabling trading*; blocking exits and cancels increases risk instead.

- **Iron Fly:** almost any live fly breaches the cap on its first exit pass, because it only takes cost-to-close × quantity above the cap (default ₹10,000). **Why it is a bug:** `unrealized_pnl` documents `entry_value` as the credit received, and every paper path sets it. The live paths leave it empty and nothing fills it in afterwards, unlike CAS Bingo. **Trigger:** Iron Fly: every live entry. Long Scalper: any live trade that is losing while the day's realized loss is near the cap.

### B-53 · A bot order that partly fills and then dies is misread: as a cancel that failed, or re-sent in full

*Added 2026-09-29, found while fixing [B-02](#b-02).*

> **Status: Fixed 2026-09-29** (not yet committed), with [B-54](#b-54). `live._DEAD` now includes both partial-fill terminal strings. A dead order that traded anything returns at once with what filled, the same as the cancelled-partial branch, and is never re-sent. The caller decides from there: the Long Scalper adopts the smaller entry, the Iron Fly and CAS Bingo unwind what filled, and an exit leaves the remainder to `held_legs`' broker-checked retries. The resolver uses the same set, so it can now settle such an order. Tests are in the "B-53 / B-54" section of `test_scalping_live.py`.

**Where:** [scalping/live.py:48 `_DEAD`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/live.py#L48), [live.py:214 `await_fill`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/live.py#L214), [live.py:373](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/live.py#L373), [order_intents.py:337 `_terminal`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/bots/scalping/order_intents.py#L337)

**What happens:**
- ICICI reports an order that traded partly and then died as **"Partially Executed And Cancelled"** or **"Partially Executed And Expired"**. These are `cfg.PARTIAL_EXECUTED_CANCELED`/`_EXPIRED`, and the order-feed parser (`order_notifications._TERMINAL_FAILURE`) already treats both as terminal. The bots' `_DEAD` set lists only `cancelled`, `canceled`, `rejected` and `expired`, matched exactly, so it misses both.
- `await_fill` therefore keeps waiting on a dead order until the fill timeout. It then tries to cancel an order that no longer exists, which is expected to fail, so `place_and_confirm` reports `cancel_failed`:
  - **Entry:** the row is frozen as pending (B-02). The resolver uses the same status set, so it cannot settle the row either. After its retries it switches the bot off, blaming an order it "could not cancel".
  - **Exit:** the Iron Fly, CAS Bingo and Long Scalper close paths halt orders for the position (`unwind_orders_halted`), leaving the unfilled remainder unprotected until the user closes it.
- If a dead order with executed units is reported under a status `_DEAD` does recognise, for example a plain `Cancelled`, the dead branch re-sends the **full** quantity and overwrites the filled count. An entry then holds more than it records. An exit sells more than is held, which on a long leg opens a naked short. Whether ICICI ever sends a plain terminal status with executed units is unverified. The code must not assume it doesn't.

**Why it is a bug:** The cancelled-partial branch a few lines below already follows the rule "a partial is not something to place more on top of". The dead-partial path breaks that rule, and the status list that decides which path runs was never matched against ICICI's real strings.

**Trigger:** An order's unfilled remainder is cancelled by someone other than the bot. That can be the user from the Order Book, or the exchange or ICICI (price-band changes, risk checks). An order expiring after a partial fill does the same.

- A stale lot size leads to rejected orders, and missing strikes to incomplete chains.
- Seen in production on 2026-09-30 with a scrip master three days old: see [B-57](#b-57) for the incident, the evidence and the proposed fix.

### B-57 · A stale scrip master leaves newly tradeable contracts with no quote, and nothing on screen says why

*Added 2026-09-30, found in production: on the Basket Order page, SENSEX 01-Oct-2026 72,000 PE priced normally while 71,900 PE showed price 0, B:S "–" and premium ₹0. This is [B-27](#b-27) observed live, plus two gaps B-27 does not cover: the tradeable flag going stale, and the silent ₹0 leg.*

**Where:**
- No freshness check on the scrip master: [cache_bootstrap.py:55 `is_reference_data_complete`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/reference_data/cache_bootstrap.py#L55), used by [scheduler.py:126 `bootstrap_reference_data_on_startup`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/reference_data/scheduler.py#L126).
- The only scheduled load: [scheduler.py:40 `_scheduler_loop`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/reference_data/scheduler.py#L40). The stale retry beside it, [`_bhavcopy_retry_due`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/reference_data/scheduler.py#L64), covers bhavcopy only.
- The tradeable filter: [scrip_index.py:198 `_liquid_contract_rows`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/reference_data/scrip_index.py#L198) (`MarginPercentage > 0`), applied when building the chain in [chain_build_service.py:145](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/chain_build_service.py#L145) and [processor.py:3108](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/processor.py#L3108).
- The readiness gate that does not see it: [chain_readiness.py:126 `_atm_window_strikes`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/chain_readiness.py#L126).
- The silent ₹0 leg: [chain-quote.ts:5 `premiumFromChainRow`](../frontend/src/lib/strategy-builder/chain-quote.ts#L5), and the `premiumPerUnit ?? 0` fallbacks in [basket-order/page.tsx:326](../frontend/src/app/basket-order/page.tsx#L326) and [page.tsx:528](../frontend/src/app/basket-order/page.tsx#L528).

**What happens:**
- A contract is subscribed on the live feed, and given a cell in the chain, only if the scrip master marks it tradeable (`MarginPercentage > 0`). That value changes from day to day: a contract that was 0 one day is positive the next.
- The scrip master is loaded only by the daily schedule (18:00 IST by default) or at startup when the cache is empty. Startup checks that the data is present, not how old it is.
- An instance that is off at 18:00 therefore never reloads it. With an old master, a contract that became tradeable since the last load is still marked non-tradeable.
- That contract is never subscribed, and its cell in the chain row is dropped. The strike still appears in the strike picker when the other side of the same strike is tradeable.
- The chain is still reported complete and "Live · WebSocket". The readiness gate checks only contracts the master calls tradeable, and only within five strikes either side of the money.
- On the Basket page the leg shows price 0, B:S "–" and premium ₹0, with no message. Net premium, max profit, max loss and breakevens are then computed with that leg at ₹0. In the incident the screen showed net premium ₹9.17 Lac for a put spread, which was the short leg's premium alone.

**Evidence from the incident** (log bundle `breeze-logs-20260930-070744-1d`, and Settings → Reference data → Ingest history):

| Observation | Value |
|---|---|
| Scrip master last ingested | 27-Sep-2026 9:44 PM IST (a Sunday), so it predates the 28 and 29 Sep sessions |
| Bhavcopy (NSE and BSE) | File date 29-Sep, ingested 30-Sep 8:00 AM IST by the stale-bhavcopy retry |
| SPAN baselines | File date 30-Sep, ingested 12:46 PM IST by their own intraday slots |
| Instance running hours | Stopped at 17:00 IST on 28 and 29 Sep (SIGTERM at 11:30 UTC), started at 08:00 IST |
| Startup on 30-Sep | `Reference data already complete in Redis; skipping startup network load` |
| Scheduled loads in the bundle | None |
| Contracts subscribed for `BFO\|BSESEN\|01-Oct-2026` | 165 |
| Tradeable contracts for that expiry in a master downloaded 30-Sep 00:22 IST | 175, including 71,900 PE (`MarginPercentage` 4,360, the lowest of the nearby puts; 72,000 PE is 424,900) |

- Running the manual scrip master refresh fixed it: 71,900 PE showed an LTP straight after.
- The 27 Sep file itself was not inspected, so the value it held for 71,900 PE was not read directly. The fix working is the confirmation.

**Why it is a bug:**
- Bhavcopy already has a staleness retry and SPAN has its own slots. The scrip master is the one reference source with neither, and it is the one that decides which contracts get a quote at all.
- Powering the instance off in the evening is a supported way to run it, so this is the normal state for those customers, not an edge case. The staleness grows by one session for every day the instance misses 18:00.
- The failure is silent in both places that could report it. The backend calls the chain complete, and the frontend treats a missing quote as a price of 0 in the payoff figures.
- The exposure is wider than the Basket page. Everything that builds a chain from the tradeable set reads the same stale list: the option chain, Strategy Builder candidates, and bots choosing strikes. Newly listed strikes and expiries, and changed lot sizes, are stale in the same way (B-27).

**Proposed solution:**
1. **Reload a stale scrip master at startup.** In `bootstrap_reference_data_on_startup`, treat the scrip master as stale when its last successful ingest is older than the close of the latest concluded trading session, and run the load even though the cache is "complete". Use `latest_concluded_trading_day`, as the bhavcopy check does, and read the ingest time from the existing ingest-history table.
2. **Give it the same retry as bhavcopy.** Extend the 30-minute retry in `_scheduler_loop` to the scrip master, under the same rule: never in market hours (design-decisions #46). This covers an instance that starts before ICICI has published the new file, and one where the 18:00 run failed.
3. **Publish it as a full load, not into the live generation.** Unlike the bhavcopy retry, a new scrip master changes the tradeable set, the token map and lot sizes, so it should go through `run_reference_data_load` and flip the version pointer once built.
4. **Show the age.** Add a warning on the Settings reference-data panel when the scrip master predates the latest concluded session, and a line in the pre-market health check. A stale master is then visible before the first trade.
5. **Stop the silent ₹0 leg.** On the Basket page, mark a leg with no quote as "No quote" instead of 0, leave it out of the payoff figures with a visible note, and block **Execute basket** for a non-aggressive leg that has no price. Check the Strategy Builder and Place Order pages for the same `?? 0` fallback.
6. **Tests.** Startup with a complete cache and a scrip ingest older than the last session triggers a load; with a fresh one it does not; the retry does not run in market hours; a basket leg with no chain cell renders "No quote" and disables execution.

**Needs a check before building:** when ICICI publishes each day's master. If it lands after 08:00 IST, a pre-market startup still gets the previous day's file and step 2's retry cannot run once the market opens. In that case the pre-open window needs its own attempt.

Fixing this also closes the scrip master half of [B-27](#b-27).

### B-56 · The frontend scores multi-leg PoP with one blended volatility, so it disagrees with the Strategy Builder

*Added 2026-09-30, found from a user question: a short strangle placed by hand showed 97.4% on Portfolio, against the 99% the Strategy Builder had proposed for strikes nearby.*

**Where:**
- The blend: [chainIv.ts:124 `blendedSigmaForLegs`](../frontend/src/lib/strategy-builder/chainIv.ts#L124), fed into [payoff.ts:404 `estimateProbabilityOfProfit`](../frontend/src/lib/strategy-builder/payoff.ts#L404).
- Its six callers:
  - [useGroupPoP.ts:44](../frontend/src/lib/portfolio/useGroupPoP.ts#L44) (Portfolio group row)
  - [useLegPoP.ts:51](../frontend/src/lib/portfolio/useLegPoP.ts#L51) (Portfolio leg row)
  - [PortfolioGroupPayoffPanel.tsx:199](../frontend/src/components/shared/payoff/PortfolioGroupPayoffPanel.tsx#L199)
  - [BasketPayoffPanel.tsx:194](../frontend/src/components/basket-order/BasketPayoffPanel.tsx#L194)
  - [StrategyPayoffPanel.tsx:123](../frontend/src/components/strategy-builder/StrategyPayoffPanel.tsx#L123)
  - [trade-metrics.ts:46](../frontend/src/lib/strategy-builder/trade-metrics.ts#L46) (used only when a proposed trade carries no backend `pop_pct`)
- The backend it should match: [pop.py:127 `pop_detail_for_legs`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/options_strategy_engine/pop.py#L127) with [iv_smile.py:82 `resolve_leg_sigma`](../backend/src/icici_breeze_backend/app/services/options_strategy_engine/iv_smile.py#L82).

**What happens:**
- Both sides compute the same thing for a short-premium structure: the probability that every short leg expires out of the money. Both build a per-side implied-volatility curve from chain quotes with a bid/ask spread of at most 10% of the mid.
- The backend then reads a separate volatility at each edge of the profit zone: the put curve at the short put strike, the call curve at the short call strike.
- The frontend instead blends the legs' volatilities into one number, weighted by quantity × premium, and uses it at every edge.
- On a skewed chain the blend gives each edge the wrong volatility. The cheaper, lower-volatility side is scored with a volatility pulled up by the other side, so its chance of finishing in the money is overstated and PoP comes out low.
- A credit spread is affected too. Only the short strike bounds its profit zone, but the long leg's volatility is blended in.
- A single leg is unaffected, because a blend of one leg is that leg's own volatility.

**Measured on a real chain** (SENSEX, expiry 01-Oct-2026, spot 72,730.62, quotes from the Strategy Builder audit file of 2026-09-30 03:58 UTC, recomputed with a standalone script):

| Short strangle | Put vol | Call vol | Per-side PoP (backend) | Blended PoP (frontend) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 69300P / 77300C (builder pick) | 26.8% | 29.5% | 99.01 | 98.97 |
| 69000P / 76500C (builder pick) | 28.4% | 25.6% | 99.00 | 98.91 |
| 69500P / 75000C (placed by hand) | 25.6% | 18.6% | 97.83 | 97.04 |

- The per-side column reproduces the two `pop_pct` values in the audit file exactly (99.01 and 99.00).
- The gap grows with the difference between the two sides' volatilities: under 0.1 points on the builder's picks, 0.8 points on the hand-placed trade.
- Most of the difference the user saw was real. The 75000 call is about 2,270 points from spot, against 3,770 to 4,570 for the builder's calls, and its chance of finishing in the money is 1.36% against 0.27% to 0.39%. The blend accounts for the remainder.

**Why it is a bug:**
- The same trade shows two PoP figures. A structure the builder accepts at the user's PoP floor can show below that floor in the payoff panel on the same page, and again on Portfolio after it is placed.
- The reason for blending no longer applies. The comment on `blendedSigmaForLegs` says one volatility is needed "because the Monte Carlo PoP shares one simulated terminal price across all legs". `estimateProbabilityOfProfit` is no longer a Monte Carlo: it evaluates a closed-form distribution at the strikes, which can take a different volatility at each one, as the backend does.

**Checked and not a cause:** the two sides measure time to expiry differently. The backend uses whole days plus one (2/365 here) and the frontend uses the time to local midnight of the expiry date, floored at 1/365. Each side uses its own value both to back out volatility and to compute PoP, so the product of volatility and the square root of time is the same, and PoP agrees to 0.01 points.

**Recommended fix:**
- Change `estimateProbabilityOfProfit` to take the per-side curves (or a function from price level and side to volatility) instead of one number. At each boundary use the put curve below spot and the call curve above it, as `pop_between_breakevens` does. All six callers then agree with the backend.
- Keep the ATM fallback for a side with fewer than two trusted quotes, as `sigmaForStrike` already does.
- In the Basket panel, apply the IV-shock slider as a multiplier on each side's volatility, not on the blend.
- Remove `blendedSigmaForLegs` once nothing else uses it.
- Add tests that pin the frontend to the backend on the three strangles above, an iron condor and a credit spread.
- Shapes with no "all shorts out of the money" zone (ratio spreads, short straddles) have breakevens between strikes. Read the volatility at each breakeven from its own side's curve, as the backend does for a straddle, and add a test that the result stays between 0 and 100 and does not jump when a strike moves by one step.
- Multi-leg PoP on Portfolio, Basket and the payoff panels will move by up to about a point. If `guide/user/` describes how PoP is computed, update it in the same change.

## Checked and not reported

- Serialized broker calls, advisory shedding, the reserved order budget and fail-closed licensing for *new* trades are intentional (#16, #24).
- Bot 2 cannot double-fire after a crash: reaped runs are coded `interrupted`, which the day gate counts as done.
- `/order` idempotency keys hold in practice, because the handler runs serially on the event loop. They would stop holding if the route were moved to a thread without adding a lock.
- Calendar helpers receive timezone-aware datetimes at every call site checked.
- The in-memory Redis `expire()` does not apply to string keys, but no current caller relies on it.
